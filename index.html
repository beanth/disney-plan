Single Pass, not Multi Pass

Radiator Springs Racers — pick one

  • Rope drop standby. Your call so far. Honest number: 2026 tracking puts the 8 am average at 101 min, because the entire park sprints to Cars Land at open. Rope drop is not a shortcut here.
  • Single rider. Over spring 2026, standby averaged 100 min while single rider ran 20–30. Splits the four of you across cars, though it often reseats groups together.
  • Single Pass. Roughly $10–25 per person on top of the Multi Pass. Decide day-of once you see the posted wait.

Reference & Strategy

🎟Multi Pass, dejargonified
  • Return window = the 1-hour arrival slot the app gives you (e.g. "come 2:40–3:40"). Not whenever you want.
  • You don't get to pick it. Unlike mobile order, there's no menu of times. You take the next available window for that ride, whatever it happens to be.
  • Which is why you book the busiest ride first. Its next-available is furthest out, and a far window is precisely where the pass saves you an hour instead of ten minutes.
  • The churn rule: one booking at a time. The next unlocks 120 minutes after you booked, or the second you tap into the ride, whichever comes first. So a distant window never costs you a rebook.
  • Rope drop what has no Lightning Lane. Peter Pan, Alice, Snow White, Mr. Toad, Pirates, Luigi's, Mater's — none are on Multi Pass, so early standby is the only cheap way on. Everything else you can buy into later.
  • Keep the wheel spinning: between headliners, book quick-loaders whose window is often "right now" — Buzz Lightyear, Star Tours, small world, Autopia at DL; Little Mermaid, Monsters Inc., Goofy's Sky School at DCA. Ride it and your next booking unlocks instantly.
  • Book at entry: you must be scanned into the park. No 7am head start — that's Florida.
  • Modify, don't cancel: refresh the "modify" screen to snag earlier return times without losing your slot.
  • Two Single Pass rides, sold separately and not included: Rise of the Resistance (DL) and Radiator Springs Racers (DCA).
  • One purchase covers both parks at Disneyland Resort.

⚠Closures & what you'll miss
  • Haunted Mansion is closed 8/8–8/20. Confirmed refurb. You lose it on 8/20 and get the Holiday version on 8/21, which is the better outcome anyway.
  • Halloween Time starts 8/21, your last park day. So 8/19 and 8/20 are pre-season — no Main Street pumpkin, no Haunted Mansion Holiday, no seasonal overlays.
  • Cars Land Haul-O-Ween. Luigi's Honkin' Haul-O-Ween, Mater's Graveyard JamBOOree, and "Radiator Screams" after dark are Halloween Time offerings. Your DCA day is 8/19. You'll probably miss all of it. Décor may be partially up since the parties began 8/18 — check the app.
  • Guardians: Monsters After Dark is the same story. Halloween Time, DCA, starts 8/21. You get the regular Mission Breakout on 8/19.
  • Madame Leota's Swinging Wake is new for 2026 but it's an Oogie Boogie Bash exclusive at DCA, so it's out either way.
  • Silly Symphony Swings (DCA) has been closed since 4/27 with no reopening date posted.
  • Frontierland Shootin' Exposition (DL) closed 6/6, no reopening date posted.
The flip side: 8/20 is the last night ever for Paint the Night and Wondrous Journeys, and 8/21 is day one of Halloween Time. You land on the exact seam between two seasons. Nobody plans that well.
Refurb calendars move. Recheck a week out — Disney announces short-notice closures on the app and their blog.
